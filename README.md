## 03 — FEATURED PROJECTS

### 🌊 01. FloodGuard AI
**River Flood Risk Intelligence & Predictive Analytics**

`Python` `Random Forest` `Scikit-learn` `Pandas` `NumPy` `Plotly` `Streamlit`

A machine learning research prototype for analyzing historical Indian river monitoring observations and predicting next-day water-level risk categories.

**Key Highlights**
- Analyzed approximately **3.86 million consecutive-day labeled observations** across 932 monitoring stations.
- Developed a Random Forest classifier for Low, Medium, and High water-level risk.
- Achieved **97.91% classification accuracy** and **0.85 High-Risk F1-score**.
- Compared performance against a persistence baseline with 98.59% accuracy.
- Built a separate model to identify transitions into High Risk, achieving 34.3% recall and 0.288 F1-score.
- Developed a Streamlit dashboard for station monitoring, historical trends, predictions, and batch CSV analysis.

**Model Performance**

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Classification Accuracy | 97.91% |
| High-Risk F1-score | 0.85 |
| Escalation Recall | 34.3% |
| Escalation F1-score | 0.288 |

> Research prototype based on historical observations. Not an official or real-time flood warning system.

[**Explore FloodGuard AI →**](https://github.com/namrataamali/FloodGuard)

### ⚡ 02. EnergyWatch AI
**Electricity Consumption Analytics & Anomaly Detection**

`Python` `Isolation Forest` `Scikit-learn` `Pandas` `Plotly` `Streamlit`

An intelligent energy analytics application designed to explore household electricity consumption patterns and identify statistically unusual usage.

**Key Highlights**
- Analyzed **34,168 hourly electricity records** from the UCI Household Electric Power Consumption dataset.
- Implemented Isolation Forest for unsupervised anomaly detection.
- Identified **1,709 statistical anomalies**, representing approximately 5% of observations.
- Obtained a **0.5853 Silhouette Score** for predicted-group separation.
- Implemented Low, Medium, and High anomaly risk categories.
- Developed an interactive dashboard with consumption trends, anomaly analysis, CSV uploads, and downloadable reports.

**Model Evaluation**

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Records Analyzed | 34,168 |
| Anomalies Identified | 1,709 |
| Anomaly Rate | 5.00% |
| Silhouette Score | 0.5853 |

> Detected anomalies represent unusual consumption patterns, not confirmed electrical faults.

[**Explore EnergyWatch AI →**](https://github.com/namrataamali?tab=repositories)

### ⚙️ 03. MachineGuard AI
**Predictive Maintenance & Equipment Failure Intelligence**

`Python` `Random Forest` `Scikit-learn` `Pandas` `Streamlit`

A predictive maintenance application that estimates equipment failure risk and supports preventive maintenance decisions through machine learning and rule-based recommendations.

**Key Highlights**
- Worked with **10,000 equipment observations** from the UCI AI4I 2020 dataset.
